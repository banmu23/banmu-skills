# Source And Quality Rules

Use these rules when materials are complex, duplicated, private, or time-sensitive.

## Fact Extraction

Extract customer materials into these fields:

1. Customer identity and positioning
2. Current business and offer
3. Target audience
4. Current channels and traffic sources
5. Current products and monetization path
6. Existing materials and proof
7. AI tool baseline and tool constraints
8. Main blockers and desired outcomes
9. Privacy, compliance, and industry risks
10. Possible next-stage service fit

If a field is missing, write `需补充` or omit it from the customer-facing plan if it would distract.

## Latest Priority

For a private workbench and consultation materials:

- Prefer the latest filename date when same-topic documents conflict.
- Prefer final/published/versioned documents over drafts.
- Treat old documents as background, evolution, or risk reminders.
- Reconfirm prices, rights, quotas, schedules, discounts, and product promises before public use.

For one session with multiple exports:

- courseware = planned path
- raw transcript = actual discussion, customer confirmation, and corrected judgment
- AI meeting note = navigation only

Do not let an AI meeting note override transcript facts.

AI summaries and meeting notes commonly fail in four ways:

- misrecognize a tool, product, person, or platform name
- combine a price with the wrong product or delivery period
- turn a conditional recommendation into a final decision
- move a future idea into the immediate action list

Verify these items against the raw transcript or the customer's latest explicit confirmation before using them in priorities, product tables, device choices, or client-facing claims.

When earlier plans exist, use this order for conflicting judgments:

1. Latest explicit customer confirmation or final action commitment
2. Latest raw transcript
3. Latest finalized/manual customized plan
4. Latest AI-generated plan
5. Intake form and older courseware
6. AI meeting notes or summaries

The lower-ranked source can still contribute facts or assets when it does not conflict. Never discard a useful earlier section merely because it is older; update its priority, timing, or wording.

Search patterns for relevant deep consultation history:

- customer industry keywords
- tool keywords: `ima`, `Workbuddy`, `Codex`, `Obsidian`, `飞书`, `知识库`, `Agent`, `Skill`
- business keywords: `获客`, `成交`, `交付`, `课程`, `读书会`, `小红书`, `公众号`, `朋友圈`, `高客单`, `OPC`

## Tool Route Heuristics

Use the lightest tool stack that can produce a real business loop. Spend most client-facing teaching and execution detail on the level the customer can reproduce now.

- **Level A, low friction:** ima copilot, Feishu, or a comparable knowledge-base tool for beginners, constrained networks, Q&A, reading, and simple content assistance.
- **Level B, operational:** Workbuddy + a plain local folder for repeatable multi-step tasks, file operations, automation, and team trials. Obsidian is optional, not a requirement.
- **Level C, durable local system:** Codex + Obsidian for a capable and willing maintainer who needs complex file work, a long-term business operating system, or Skill productization.
- **Feishu knowledge base / docs:** use as the company/team main workspace when visibility, permissions, delivery docs, or cloud collaboration matter.
- **Skill / Agent workflow:** only after a manual loop has produced repeatable steps, accepted examples, and quality standards.

Upgrade gates:

- `Level A -> Level B`: one high-frequency task has stable inputs, steps, output, and acceptance line.
- `manual workflow -> Skill`: at least one accepted final, revision reasons, forbidden patterns, and QA checks exist.
- `Level B -> Level C`: the customer has enough material, maintenance willingness, and business complexity to justify it.

For founder/team cases, tools may differ but the knowledge base, accepted examples, and quality standards must stay aligned.

## Benchmark And Evidence Validation

Use benchmarks to test whether the proposed audience, problem, content entry, offer, and result already have market evidence. Do not use them for copying.

- A matching validated model can strengthen a P0 candidate.
- No benchmark does not prove the idea is impossible, but it raises the evidence burden.
- Treat an unsupported direction as a hypothesis. Run a small content, consultation, preorder, or sample-delivery test before committing the main positioning or product line.
- Prefer the customer's already-run business, accepted delivery, paid result, or strong professional asset over a fashionable but unsupported AI direction.

For every major recommendation, require a practical proof chain when applicable:

1. real source input
2. core operation
3. process screenshot or representative sample
4. visible result
5. acceptance criteria
6. human-review or risk boundary

Theory volume, prompt count, tool count, and knowledge-base page count are not proof.

## Skill Eligibility And Granularity

Do not make a Skill for every task. A Skill candidate should be:

- frequent enough to justify reuse
- stable enough to describe
- explicit about inputs and outputs
- backed by at least one accepted final or qualified sample
- testable with forbidden patterns and a QA line
- valuable for repeated personal, team, or customer use

Keep exploratory, low-frequency, or highly contextual personal work as project rules, knowledge, or SOP.

Split Skills at real quality boundaries:

- separate when a split materially improves output quality, recovery, or human confirmation
- keep the client-facing delivery to one entry and normally two or three visible steps at most
- allow more modular internal orchestration behind that simple entry
- give every Skill an input contract, output contract, acceptance line, forbidden patterns, and escalation path

## Human And AI Ownership

For IP, content, courses, consulting, and professional services, define three zones:

- **Human-authored/judged:** core experience, values, real stories, key cases, professional judgment, and final business decisions.
- **AI-assisted:** organization, expansion, rewriting, classification, first drafts, visualization, and QA assistance.
- **Mandatory final human review:** facts, voice, professional risks, client promises, and public release.

Authenticity is part of delivery quality. Do not frame full automation as the default success condition.

## Build Buy Partner Or Wait

Before proposing a custom app, website, or enterprise workbench, verify:

- repeated demand exists
- a manual/service MVP has been accepted
- target users will use it repeatedly
- inputs, outputs, and data boundaries are stable
- a maintenance owner exists
- willingness to pay or measurable business value exists

If these gates are missing, prefer trying, distributing, partnering on, or co-creating an existing solution.

For a page, dashboard, workbench, or app that stores customer or business data, also verify:

- source file and data locations are known
- demo data is separated from real data
- export backup succeeds
- restore from backup succeeds
- versions are numbered
- upgrades do not overwrite historical data
- customer data is isolated
- privacy and maintenance ownership are explicit

Until these pass, position the artifact as a Demo or customized service MVP, not a finished general product.

For enterprise cases, select one primary workspace/source of truth. Use other Agents only to fill a clear capability gap. For email, quotes, contracts, policy interpretations, and client promises, default phase one to `AI draft -> human check -> human send/confirm`.

## Cross-Device And External-Resource Boundaries

File synchronization does not automatically synchronize:

- chat context
- Agent runtime state
- installed Skills
- local permissions
- account login state
- platform-side workflows

Define one source of truth, versioning, backup, and conflict handling for cross-device work.

Before productizing any external Skill, code, template, image, course, or dataset, verify source, license, attribution, modification rights, commercial-use rights, redistribution rights, privacy, and platform terms. Do not remove watermarks, repackage without permission, scrape private data, share or buy unknown accounts/session keys, or automate behavior that evades platform controls.

If a tool, product, or model name cannot be mapped confidently to its current official name or capability, mark it `需核验`. Live-verify decision-critical versions, capabilities, prices, and rules.

## Business Diagnosis Checklist

Do not diagnose "AI tool problem" too early. Check whether the true bottleneck is:

- unclear customer positioning
- weak content entry
- offer too hard to understand
- product ladder missing
- proof materials scattered
- customer education too expensive
- no follow-up system
- no delivery SOP
- team cannot replicate founder experience
- compliance risk or platform risk

Before selecting P0, score the candidate first loops qualitatively against:

- direct business value
- existing source assets
- customer urgency
- customer willingness and stated next action
- available time and delivery load
- device/network/tool readiness
- ability to produce a visible result within 7 days
- value as a reusable SOP/Skill sample

The winner is not always the nearest-to-revenue loop. Prefer the highest-value loop the customer can actually start and complete now.

When the customer has proven high-performing content, sales, renewals, referrals, or mature operations, treat those as first-class P0 evidence. Use AI to reproduce and standardize the win before asking the customer to abandon it for a new direction.

Keep public positioning and backstage capability separate. Public positioning should normally lead with the customer, problem, and result; AI, Agent, and Skill explain how the result becomes more efficient or differentiated. Only foreground AI when the audience is buying AI learning or peer enablement. Evaluate whether a product is current through target-customer adoption and outcomes, not AI-industry novelty alone.

When the customer has recordings, workshops, consultation records, courseware, templates, or accepted final examples, include an existing-asset-first candidate. A practical course MVP can follow:

1. choose one narrow course result
2. design a three-level outline
3. map existing recordings to lessons
4. label each source as direct use / edit and use / extract case only / do not use
5. manually produce three to five short sample lessons before promising automated editing
6. test in a permission-controlled knowledge base or delivery space
7. use learner feedback to update the outline, delivery, SOP, and later Skill

If the customer lacks stable course or service evidence, use service-first packaging instead:

1. one real audience
2. one concrete task
3. one complete delivery
4. one user feedback cycle
5. several improved deliveries
6. only then a curriculum, training camp, poster, or standardized rights

Do not substitute recording a large course for demand and delivery validation.

## Startability And Troubleshooting Gate

For complex or beginner plans, finish with a 72-hour start card containing:

- one first action
- one real source input
- one tool/location
- one visible deliverable
- one acceptance line
- one support route and the screenshot/information needed
- an explicit pause list

If the customer cannot restate the first action in their own words, the plan is still overloaded.

For tool-blocked customers, troubleshoot in this order:

`environment/network -> official source/version -> account/login -> least privilege -> model/quota -> file read/write -> one real-task acceptance test`

Opening an interface is not completion. Do not default to full access and do not request passwords or session keys.

When a customer operates two business lines, compare audience, promise, account, knowledge base, and compliance. Separate the lines when those differ materially; merge only after a shared customer journey is proven.

## Execution Completeness

For each major recommendation, verify all applicable fields:

- `why now`
- source inputs
- exact action sequence
- visible deliverable
- owner/operator
- acceptance criteria
- risk or human-review boundary
- upgrade/stop condition
- knowledge/SOP/Skill feedback destination

For multi-question customers, build an internal coverage matrix before drafting and a client-visible coverage table when it improves clarity.

For substantial plans, use two time horizons:

- **7 days:** setup plus one visible small loop
- **30 days:** staged expansion, testing, feedback, and first reusable asset

Do not define the 30-day goal as building every tool, knowledge base, course, Skill, and content system.

## Client-Visible Safety

Never expose:

- private customer screenshots or chat details without permission
- local filesystem paths
- internal creator notes
- customer income, payment, backend data, or private business numbers
- unverified testimonials, results, or before/after claims
- medical, legal, financial, or education claims without proper boundaries

For regulated/high-stakes industries, check current official or primary sources when giving compliance guidance.

At minimum, state what AI may assist with, what requires qualified human review, what sensitive data cannot enter public models, and what remains an unresolved risk. Never convert an unresolved source question into a confident answer.

Treat historical third-party account registration, network-access, recharge, payment, and unofficial service links as sensitive and unstable. Do not copy them into a new plan, SOP, Skill, or customer package without current safety, legality, and currency verification.

Consultation materials may support an anonymized case, article, social post, oral script, course update, or Skill improvement only after the customer deliverable is complete and permission plus desensitization are handled. Never turn raw private consultation material directly into public content.

## Output Self-Check

Before finalizing, verify:

- The plan references the customer's actual facts.
- The first-stage path is narrow enough to execute.
- There is a clear "do now / pause / later" judgment.
- The first priority reflects the latest customer-confirmed readiness.
- Each major phase has a deliverable, acceptance line, and upgrade gate.
- The AI tools match constraints and maturity.
- The plan focuses on the tool level the customer can reproduce now; Level B does not require Obsidian by default.
- Unsupported positioning/product ideas are tested before becoming P0.
- Major recommendations include real evidence and acceptance criteria.
- Proposed Skills pass the frequency, stability, accepted-example, QA, and reuse test.
- Skill splits follow quality boundaries while keeping customer operation simple.
- Human judgment, AI assistance, and final review are explicit where authenticity or professional responsibility matters.
- Custom-build recommendations pass demand, use, data, payment/value, and maintenance gates.
- Existing recordings and other reusable assets were considered before recommending from-scratch production.
- The plan includes a knowledge base or workflow structure when useful.
- The plan includes a 7-day action path.
- A complex plan includes a 30-day progression and final immediate actions.
- Every high-priority customer question maps to a solution, deliverable, or explicit unresolved item.
- The first real workflow covers Plan, complete first output, trial, accepted final, feedback review, and SOP/Skill update.
- Team cases include shared knowledge, accepted examples, QA, escalation, and Skill feedback updates.
- The plan includes prompts or templates if they reduce friction.
- Client-ready mode contains no internal meeting or delivery language.
- Missing information is handled without fabrication.
- AI summaries were not used as sole proof for tool names, prices, periods, device decisions, or customer commitments.
- Source-file sync was not confused with chat, Agent, Skill, permission, or runtime sync.
- External resources passed license, attribution, commercial-use, redistribution, privacy, and platform-term checks.
- Complex or beginner plans end with one 72-hour start card and a pause list.
- Understanding, installation, folder creation, or page opening was not treated as a real outcome.
- Proven customer wins were considered before new tools and new directions.
- Public positioning leads with audience, problem, and result unless AI itself is the product.
- New courses and services without stable evidence use service-first validation.
- Data-bearing AI products pass persistence, export/restore, version, upgrade, isolation, privacy, and maintenance gates.
- Tool troubleshooting closes on a repeatable real task with least privilege.
- Unrelated business lines are not forced into one account, offer, or knowledge base.
