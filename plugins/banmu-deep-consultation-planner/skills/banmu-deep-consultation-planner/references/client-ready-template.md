# Client-Ready Deep Consultation Plan Template

Use this template for customer-facing plans. Adapt headings to the customer, but keep the logic: diagnose, choose path, make it executable.

## Title

`YYYYMMDD 客户名丨AI落地方案` or `客户名丨AI落地方案`

Avoid "深度咨询备课", "腾讯会议", "演示流程", "内部提醒", and other internal terms in client-visible titles.

## Opening Judgment

Start with a short blockquote or first paragraph that gives the key path judgment:

```text
核心判断：你现在最该先解决的不是【工具/复杂系统】，而是先用【最适合当前阶段的工具】跑通【最短业务闭环】。等这个闭环稳定后，再升级到【下一阶段工具/工作流/系统】。
```

The opening must answer three questions without warm-up: what the customer already has, what the real bottleneck is, and what the first executable loop should be. When the latest transcript changed an earlier plan, write only the corrected final judgment here.

If the customer already has high-performing content, real sales, renewals, referrals, or a mature workflow, name that proven win and explain how the first phase will reproduce it. Do not let a new tool erase the customer's strongest evidence.

## Recommended Structure

### 一、你当前最重要的业务判断

Write directly to the customer. Include:

- Who they are and what stage they are in.
- Their strongest real assets.
- What is not yet connected into a system.
- The real bottleneck in business terms.
- The shortest path for this stage.

### 二、最终执行顺序

For complex cases, add a stage table. P0-P4 is recommended but not mandatory.

| 阶段 | 唯一重点 | 可见交付物 | 进入条件 | 完成/升级条件 |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Follow it with a concise `现在做 / 先暂停 / 以后升级` judgment. Do not let the customer start multiple systems at once.

### 三、AI 提效路线

Name the recommended tool route by stage.

Examples:

- `A 层先用 ima / 飞书跑通一个任务，B 层再用 Workbuddy + 本地文件夹，达到维护门槛后再进入 C 层 Codex + Obsidian`
- `先飞书知识库建立团队主阵地，再用 Workbuddy 补高频流程，稳定后沉淀 SOP / Skill`
- `创始人有维护能力时用 Codex + Obsidian 自用，员工和客户保留更低门槛入口`

Explain why each stage fits the customer's current constraints.

When the founder and team have different capabilities, show a dual route:

- Founder: deeper local system for rule refinement and Skill iteration
- Team / customer: lower-friction Agent for repeatable execution
- Shared base: permissioned knowledge base, accepted examples, and QA rules

State the upgrade condition for each stage. Do not require Obsidian for the Workbuddy + local-folder level unless it creates clear value for this customer. Put most execution detail on the level the customer can reproduce now.

Also evaluate device, network, company policy, available time, learning capacity, team skill, and maintenance willingness when they affect the tool route. Do not recommend a device or platform that breaks the customer's core professional workflow.

### 四、第一阶段的唯一主线

Give one focused path:

```text
用【工具】搭建【客户自己的业务知识库 / 内容系统 / 交付系统】，先跑通“素材沉淀 -> 内容输出 -> 客户教育 -> 产品承接”的最小闭环。
```

Adapt the loop to the customer's business.

If the customer already has recordings, workshops, consultation records, courseware, templates, or accepted samples, first evaluate whether an existing-asset-first MVP is faster and safer than producing everything again. For a course case, a useful route is `three-level outline -> recording-to-lesson mapping -> three to five short sample lessons -> permission-controlled trial -> learner feedback -> stable course -> course-making Skill`.

Write the first loop as a real SOP:

```text
真实输入 -> Plan 模式 -> 客户/创始人确认 -> 完整第一版 -> 小范围试跑 -> 满意终稿 -> 差异复盘 -> SOP / Skill / 知识库更新
```

For every step, name the input, output, person responsible, acceptance line, and risk boundary when useful.

For every major recommendation, show a proof chain where applicable:

```text
真实输入 -> 核心操作 -> 截图 / 样例 -> 可见结果 -> 验收标准 -> 人工复核边界
```

When the first direction lacks matching benchmarks, paid evidence, or accepted delivery, state the smallest validation test before treating it as the main line.

For public positioning, lead with the target customer, concrete problem, and desired result. Keep AI / Agent / Skill as the backstage production method unless the target customer is explicitly buying AI learning or peer enablement. A product can still be valuable when it is not new to AI practitioners if the target customer can understand, use, and benefit from it.

When a new course or service lacks stable delivery evidence, use a service-first MVP: one audience, one task, one complete delivery, feedback, several improved deliveries, and only then a standardized curriculum or broad rights.

When Skill productization is relevant, explain why the task qualifies: it is frequent, stable, has clear inputs/outputs, accepted examples, forbidden patterns, a QA line, and real reuse value. Keep exploratory work as knowledge/SOP. Keep customer operation to one entry and normally two or three visible steps; split internal Skills only at quality, recovery, or human-confirmation boundaries.

For IP, content, course, consulting, or professional work, distinguish what must come from the customer, what AI may assist, and what requires final human review.

### 五、知识库 / 工作台搭建结构

Provide a concrete directory or module structure. Keep it small enough to execute immediately. Common modules:

- 个人说明书 / IP说明书
- 产品与服务资料
- 内容素材
- 客户问答与异议库
- 案例与反馈
- 课程 / 交付 / SOP
- 内容选题池
- 合规与禁用表达

Use the smallest structure needed for the first loop. Explicitly say what not to migrate or organize yet. If the customer has scattered historical materials, apply `用到什么整理什么` rather than requiring a full cleanup before execution.

### 六、产品 / 内容 / 获客 / 转化建议

Translate customer assets into content pillars, public-channel strategy, private-domain follow-up, or product education path. Do not make generic channel advice; tie every recommendation to the customer's existing materials and audience.

One strong consultation or delivery record may support an anonymized case, article, social post, oral script, course update, and Skill improvement, but only after the original customer deliverable is complete and permission plus desensitization are handled.

When product information is complex, add a product ladder table with customer, problem, result, delivery, price/status, and confirmation risk. Use only confirmed prices and clearly label fields that require current confirmation.

Before recommending a custom app, website, or enterprise workbench, test repeated demand, a manual/service MVP, sustained use, data boundaries, maintenance ownership, and willingness to pay or measurable business value. When these are missing, prefer trying, distributing, partnering on, or co-creating an existing product.

For an AI page, dashboard, or workbench that stores data, also require successful export and restore, version numbering, upgrade compatibility, real/demo data separation, customer data isolation, privacy, and a maintenance owner. Before those checks pass, call it a Demo or customized service MVP rather than a finished general product.

### 七、客户问题覆盖表

For customers with multiple stated concerns, include a coverage table:

| 客户关切 | 最终解决方式 | 所在阶段 | 核心交付物 | 验收标准 / 待补闭环 |
|---|---|---|---|---|

This prevents a strong narrative from silently omitting one of the customer's actual questions.

### 八、产品与变现闭环

Build a ladder only when the customer's materials support it:

1. Free or low-friction trust entry
2. Low-threshold educational entry
3. Middle product / course / consultation
4. High-value service / membership / co-creation
5. Long-term system asset / team replication

Do not invent offers, prices, outcomes, or rights.

### 九、7 天启动与 30 天推进

Make actions concrete by day or by time window:

- 当天: one setup action and one visible output
- 3 天内: one small loop
- 7 天内: one demonstrable result and one review

For substantial cases, also provide four weekly stages. Each week must have a visible output and acceptance line. End with a short 30-day acceptance statement; do not define success as "all systems completed."

For complex or beginner cases, finish with a 72-hour start card:

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 第一件事 | 只写一个动作 |
| 真实输入 | 指定一份资料 / 一个客户任务 |
| 工具位置 | 在哪里完成 |
| 可见交付物 | 必须能看见、能发出或能复用 |
| 完成标准 | 客户怎样判断完成 |
| 卡住时 | 提供什么截图 / 信息，去哪里反馈 |
| 本阶段暂停 | 明确不做的工具、产品或平台 |

Do not count understanding, installation, folder creation, or opening a page as the final result. The customer should be able to restate the first action in their own words.

### 十、团队复制方案（有团队时）

Include:

- One narrow pilot task
- Complete first draft before segment-by-segment corrections
- Accepted final example and revision reasons
- Small focused Skill rather than a broad assistant
- Shared, desensitized knowledge base
- Employee trial run and founder-versus-team comparison
- QA checklist, escalation rule, and feedback update

### 十一、可复制口令

Include prompt blocks only when they make execution easier. Prompt blocks should reference the customer's own knowledge base and forbid fabrication.

For complex plans, normally include prompts for the first output, the first workflow, personal/brand instruction extraction, final-draft feedback, and Skill extraction when relevant. Prompts should tell the Agent to plan first, state fixed outputs, mark missing facts, and wait for confirmation before large writes.

### 十二、风险与边界

Include compliance, privacy, data, platform, medical, financial, legal, education, income-claim, or industry-specific risks when relevant. Use official or customer-provided sources when claims are high stakes.

For regulated or sensitive work, distinguish:

- AI-assisted draft / organization work
- Mandatory professional human review
- Data that must be removed, masked, or kept out of public models
- Permission, logging, deletion, contract, qualification, and current-rule checks

For email, quotes, contracts, policy interpretations, and customer promises, default the first phase to `AI 生成草稿 -> 人工核对 -> 人工发送 / 确认`.

For recordings or student/customer materials, add consent, masking, identifiable audio/video, enterprise data, and public-use boundaries.

Do not copy historical third-party account, network-access, recharge, registration, or payment links into the plan unless their current safety, legality, and validity have been verified.

Before using external Skills, templates, code, images, courses, or datasets in a customer product, verify source, license, attribution, modification, commercial-use, redistribution, privacy, and platform terms. Do not remove watermarks, repackage without permission, scrape private data, use unknown shared accounts/session keys, or automate behavior that evades platform controls.

When cross-device or sync is involved, state that source-file sync does not automatically sync chat context, Agent runtime, installed Skills, permissions, login state, or platform workflows. Define one source of truth, versioning, backup, and conflict handling.

For tool troubleshooting, use `环境 / 网络 -> 官方来源和版本 -> 账号登录 -> 最小权限 -> 模型 / 额度 -> 文件读写 -> 一个真实任务复测`. Do not default to full access or ask the customer for passwords / session keys.

If the customer operates two business lines with different audiences, promises, knowledge, accounts, or compliance, recommend separation first. Merge only when a shared customer journey has been demonstrated.

If a tool/product/model name or current capability cannot be confirmed, label it `需核验` rather than repeating an AI meeting-note guess.

If the available source only raises a risk and does not solve it, label it as an open closure item.

### 十三、下一阶段升级方向

State what to do after the first loop is stable:

- Add Workbuddy workflow
- Turn repeatable actions into SOP
- Build a Skill / Agent workflow
- Build team replication assets
- Upgrade to a heavier AI business system

Keep the tone natural. Do not hard sell.

### 十四、现在只开始这几步

Close with three to five immediate actions in order. Each action should be small enough to begin now and should point to a concrete deliverable.

## Granularity Guardrails

- Do not force every plan into the same chapter count. Complex cases may naturally reach 12-16 core sections; simple cases should stay shorter.
- Depth comes from decisions, gates, deliverables, prompts, and acceptance criteria, not from repeating the diagnosis.
- Every major recommendation must answer `why now`, `what exactly to produce`, and `how to know it is complete`.
- Preserve useful earlier-plan content, but rewrite the final structure around the latest confirmed P0.
- Do not mention earlier drafts, transcript corrections, or meeting history in the client-visible document.

## Client-Ready Language Rules

Use:

- "你现在最该先做的是..."
- "第一阶段先求跑通，不求复杂"
- "等这个动作稳定后，再升级..."
- "这一步的价值是..."

Avoid:

- "本场咨询"
- "腾讯会议"
- "演示"
- "现场"
- "半目会前补充"
- "客户当前状态判断" if the client will see it; use "你当前..."
- "高客单承接判断" if visible; use "下一阶段升级方向"
- Internal product upsell language unless the user explicitly wants a sales version
